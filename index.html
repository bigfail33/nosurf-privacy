NoSurf Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026 · Developer: NoSurf

Overview

NoSurf is a local-first digital-wellbeing and productivity application. It does not provide medical or mental-health advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Information accessed and stored

If you voluntarily enable Android Usage Access, NoSurf accesses app package names, app display names, and timestamped foreground, background, screen-interactive, and keyguard events supplied by Android. It uses these events to calculate daily foreground-app time and per-app duration and launch totals. Android may make these events available periodically while NoSurf is not open.

NoSurf stores computed daily and per-app duration and launch totals, goals, app limits, focus-session records, optional self-reported reflections, and preferences locally on your device. Raw usage events are processed locally and are not retained. NoSurf does not read messages, passwords, photos, typed content, or the content viewed inside other apps.

Collection, sharing, and third parties

The current NoSurf release has no internet permission, advertising, analytics, account system, or cloud-sync SDK. NoSurf does not transmit, sell, or share usage data, plans, reflections, or identifiers with the developer or third parties. A user-initiated CSV export is written only to the destination the user chooses through Android's document picker.

Google Play may independently provide the developer with aggregated installation, crash, and performance information under Google's own terms and privacy policy. That information is generated by Google Play, not transmitted by NoSurf's application code.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain on the device until you delete them in NoSurf's Settings, clear the app's storage, or uninstall NoSurf. Android Usage Access history is controlled separately by Android. NoSurf disables Android cloud backup for app-owned data.

Your choices

Usage Access is optional. Focus timers, plans, and reflections work without it. You can decline during onboarding, grant or revoke access in Android Settings, export a summary, skip any reflection, and delete all NoSurf-owned local data from Settings.

Children

NoSurf is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If NoSurf's data practices change, this policy, the in-app notice, and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated before the changed release is distributed.
